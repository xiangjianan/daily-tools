How it works

Each character is mapped to its physical QWERTY key. Uppercase and shifted symbols add a visit to a Shift key. Travel distance walks your fingers along real key positions (1u = 19.05 mm) in typed order. It's a model of touch typing, not a recording — nothing ever leaves this page.

Runs 100% offline in your browser — no upload, no account, no analytics. Detection is regex-based and deliberately conservative: phones require a leading + country code, card numbers must pass the Luhn checksum, long tokens must look like hex or base64. It is a helper, not an auditor — always skim the result before posting. Names and free-text identities are out of scope.
